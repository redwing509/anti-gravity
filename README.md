# Anti-Gravity

Model-only, evidence-first investigation of anti-gravity and propellantless-thrust claims.

This is a research record, not a claim that anti-gravity is an established physical effect and not a construction-ready device. No known, experimentally established physics produces a usable anti-gravity force. The project tests published claims against their own measurements, independent replications, and what mainstream theory actually permits.

## Scope

The work examines claims such as:

- Biefeld-Brown / electrohydrodynamic (ionic wind) thrust
- Podkletnov rotating-superconductor gravity-modification claims
- Woodward / Mach-effect thruster claims
- EmDrive results, including the reported TU Dresden 2021 null tests
- Martin Tajmar gravitomagnetic and related laboratory claims
- What general relativity and quantum field theory are stated to allow, including negative energy, frame dragging, and energy-condition constraints

A measured ion-wind force, a thermal artifact, or a frame-dragging effect predicted by general relativity is not evidence of a new anti-gravity force. Numerical agreement between solvers is not experimental validation.

## Compute and results

Work is model-only and uses free compute only. No paid compute is provisioned automatically. Simulation outputs belong on a `simulation-results` branch; they are evidence, not validation. Promotion onto `main` requires at least two independent engines agreeing within about 5% on a matched case, a pull request, and James's approval.

## Contribute and review

Ideas, corrections, references, models and code may be submitted as an issue or a pull request. Submission is not acceptance. After this scaffold, every change goes through a branch and a pull request that James reviews. Do not invent citations. Do not use Sci-Hub, Library Genesis, or similar sites. Installing any simulation engine needs a separate OK.
